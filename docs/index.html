The three theorems

  • First: any chart complete enough to explain the pump contains at least one candle it cannot justify.
  • Second: no trader can prove their own consistency from inside their own P&L.
  • Third (unpublished): the terminal was always the product. The coin is a corollary.
“This token is not provable within this token.” — GodelCoin.theorem()

Contract surface

totalSupply()          -> 1_931_000_000e18, fixed at deploy
transfer/approve/transferFrom/allowance   standard ERC-20
burn(uint256)          -> your tokens only, supply goes down
isComplete()           -> false
isConsistent()         -> true          // you cannot verify this from in here
godelNumber(address)   -> keccak("G(" || holder || ")")
theorem()              -> the undecidable sentence

// absent by design: mint, owner, pause, blacklist, setFee, upgradeTo

The absences are the whole pitch. There is no second contract, no upgrade path, and no privileged address — a memecoin you can finish reading before you decide not to buy it.

Two variants

GodelCoin.sol is a normal fixed-supply ERC-20 — transferable, therefore tradable. GodelCoinBound.sol is the same joke with the exit welded shut: the whole supply is minted once to one immutable holder, and transfer, transferFrom and approve revert unconditionally — for the holder as much as for anyone else. Even a zero-value transfer reverts, so a DEX router probing the token fails before it can pool it. No market, no price, nobody to be hurt by it. A coin that can only be held and proven, never exchanged.
